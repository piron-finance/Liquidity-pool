# 📑 Piron Pools — Feature Document

## 1. Product Categories

### A. **Managed Pools (stable yield pools)**

* ** Piron Emerging Markets Pool(for emerging markets, ngn, kes, ghs, turkey etc)**

  * Assets: Short-term sovereign bills (≤6 months) across emerging markets (NGN, KES, GHS, etc.).
  * Durations(users can choose): 90d, 180d, 270d, 360d.
  * Redemption:

    * Minimum 30 days invested before break(cliff period).
    * Early break allowed with penalty fee; settlement T+0/1 .
  * Yield: Published APY per tenor (e.g., 90d = 8%, 180d = 9.5%, 360d = 11%)(we need to ponder on the math to calculate this).
  * NAV updated daily/weekly.

* ** Piron Dollar Pool**

  * Assets: US T-bills ≤6 months.
  * Same rules as above.
  * Target market: dollarized emerging market investors + global stablecoin users.

* ** Piron  Bond Pools **

  * Assets: Interest-bearing sovereign bonds (longer maturities than bills).
  * Redemption: lockups aligned to bond maturity ladders (e.g., 1y–3y buckets).
  * Use-case: For investors comfortable with longer duration and stable sovereign credit.

---

### B. **Single-Asset Pools (Non-sovereign credit instruments)**

* **Commercial Paper Pools**

  * Assets: Short-term corporate debt (30–270 days).
  * Nature: Higher yield, higher risk than Tbills.
  * Redemption: At maturity (fixed lock pools).
  * Target market: Yield-seeking retail/institutional investors.

* **Invoice Financing Pools**

  * Assets: Factored invoices (30–120 days).
  * Redemption: At maturity.
  * Transparency: Show borrower, invoice counterparty, expected repayment.

* **Private Credit Pools** (longer term SME/corporate loans).

  * Assets: 6–24 month loans.
  * Redemption: maturity only; possible early exit only via secondary market.
  * Yield: higher, but disclosed risk.

* **(Optional future)** Managed Credit Pools → diversified baskets of CP, invoices, SME loans.

---

### C. **Future: Piron Lending Layer**

* Pools as collateral:

  * Users holding Piron pool shares (e.g., Stable Yield Pool tokens, CP Pool tokens) can borrow stablecoins against them.
  * Loan-to-Value (LTV) based on pool type:

    * Tbill pools (government): high LTV (60–80%).
    * CP/invoice pools: lower LTV (30–50%).
  * If user defaults, Piron liquidates their pool tokens.

* Product flow:

  * User has 10k USDC in Stable Yield Pool.
  * Borrows 5k USDC against it without selling → maintains yield exposure while accessing liquidity.

---

## 2. Redemption & Liquidity Rules

* **Tbill / Sovereign Pools**

  * Duration options: 90/180/270/360 days.
  * Minimum hold: 30 days.
  * Early exit penalty: 3-5% haircut, depending on tenor.
  * Settlement:

    * T+0/1 if liquidity buffer covers.
    * Otherwise, queued until nearest maturity.

* **Credit / CP / Invoice Pools**

  * Fixed maturity lock.
  * No early redemption (or only via OTC/secondary market).
  * Payout at maturity.

---

## 3. Yield Calculation

* **Published APY / Tenor yields**

  * Example (illustrative):

    * 90d: 8.0% APY
    * 180d: 9.5% APY
    * 360d: 11.0% APY

* **How returns accrue**

  * Yield accrues daily on user shares (NAV ↑ daily).
  * Redemption = `shares * NAV_at_redeem`.
  * For early exits, penalty applied → deducted from NAV payout.

* **Transparency**

  * Show user-specific yield projections at entry.
  * Show live NAV updates + historical yield chart.

---

## 4. User-Facing Features

* **Pool Dashboard**

  * AUM, current APY, duration options.
  * Asset breakdown (Tbills, bonds, CPs, invoices).
  * Historical performance graph.
  * Redemption status (available now / queued / locked).

* **Flexible choice of tenor**

  * Users can select 90d / 180d / 270d etc.
  * UX: “Lock for 90 days → get estimated 8% APY.”

* **Early Exit Option**

  * Available after 30 days.
  * Clear penalty disclosed before confirming.

* **Risk Disclosure**

  * Stable Yield Pool = government-backed.
  * CP/Invoice Pools = corporate credit risk.

---

## 5. Institutional-Facing Features

* **Bespoke Pools**

  * Custom mandate pools (e.g., “USD 50m laddered 90/180d Tbill strategy”).

* **Tranching**

  * Senior / junior tranches within CP/credit pools.
  * Senior gets first-loss protection, junior gets higher yield.

* **API / Reporting**

  * Daily NAV API.
  * Portfolio breakdown, maturity ladder, concentration.

* **Custodial Integration**

  * Support Fireblocks, Anchorage, Coinbase Custody.

---

## 6. Roadmap

**Phase 1 (MVP: 3–6 months)**

* Launch Stable Yield Pools (Emerging Markets, Dollar).
* Single-asset CP / Invoice Pools.
* Dashboard + NAV updates.
* Early exit penalty system.

**Phase 2 (6–12 months)**

* Sovereign Bond Pools (longer maturities).
* Managed Credit Pools (basket of CP/invoices).
* Institutional reporting APIs.
* Fiat/stablecoin ramps.

**Phase 3 (12–18 months)**

* Lending layer: borrow against pool shares.
* Senior/junior tranching for CP/invoice pools.
* Cross-border products (multi-currency yield pools).
* Whitelabel distribution to banks/fintechs.

---

✅ **Summary vision**:

* Retail: **Stable Yield Pools** (government paper) + **Locked Pools** (CP, invoice, private credit).
* Institutions: **Bespoke managed pools, tranching, reporting APIs, collateralized lending.**
* Future: Piron evolves into a **yield + lending platform**, where users don’t just earn yield but can also unlock liquidity on top of their positions.
