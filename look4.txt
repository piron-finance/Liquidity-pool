# 📑 Piron Yield Calculation Framework

## 1. Principles

1. **Transparency**: Users see both (a) a quoted APY for their lock tenor and (b) their live accrued return.
2. **Consistency**: Yield is derived from the actual performance of the underlying T-Bill pools (weighted average), not arbitrary numbers.
3. **Flexibility**: Works for multiple lock durations (90d, 180d, 360d) even if backend runs one managed pool with laddered holdings.
4. **Fairness**: Early exits get accrued yield pro-rated to days invested, minus penalty.

---

## 2. Inputs

* **Underlying pool composition**: list of all T-Bill holdings, with:

  * Purchase price $P_i$
  * Face value $FV_i$
  * Days to maturity $T_i$
  * Allocation weight $w_i$ (proportion of pool AUM)

* **Current date**: for yield accrual and early exit calculations.

---

## 3. Instrument-level Yield

For each instrument $i$:

$$
Yield_i = \frac{FV_i - P_i}{P_i}
$$

$$
APY_i = \left(\frac{FV_i}{P_i} - 1\right) \times \frac{365}{T_i}
$$

---

## 4. Pool Average APY

Weighted average APY across all instruments:

$$
APY_{pool} = \sum_i (w_i \times APY_i)
$$

This gives you the **baseline annualized yield** of the pool.

---

## 5. Pro-rated Tenor APY

For a lock tenor $L$ (in days):

$$
Return_L = (1 + APY_{pool})^{\frac{L}{365}} - 1
$$

$$
APY_L = \frac{Return_L \times 365}{L}
$$

* This ensures that 90d lock shows a slightly lower APY (annualized from shorter holding).
* 360d lock shows close to the true pool APY.

---

## 6. User Yield Accrual

* User deposits amount $A$ at time $t_0$.
* Daily accrual rate:

$$
DailyRate = (1 + APY_{pool})^{\frac{1}{365}} - 1
$$

* After $d$ days:

$$
AccruedValue = A \times (1 + DailyRate)^d
$$

---

## 7. Early Exit

* **Minimum hold**: 30 days.
* If user exits at day $d < L$:

$$
Redemption = AccruedValue \times (1 - Penalty)
$$

Where **Penalty** = 2-5% (configurable).

* If buffer liquidity insufficient → redemption may be queued until liquidity available.

---

## 8. Example Calculation

**Pool composition**:

* 50% 90d Tbills at 8% APY
* 50% 180d Tbills at 9.5% APY

$$
APY_{pool} = 0.5 \times 8\% + 0.5 \times 9.5\% = 8.75\%
$$

**Published tenors**:

* 90d lock:

  $$
  Return_{90} = (1+0.0875)^{90/365} - 1 \approx 2.14\%
  $$

  $$
  APY_{90} \approx 8.7\%
  $$

* 180d lock:

  $$
  Return_{180} \approx 4.38\% 
  $$

  $$
  APY_{180} \approx 8.9\%
  $$

* 360d lock:

  $$
  Return_{360} \approx 9.14\%
  $$

  $$
  APY_{360} \approx 9.14\%
  $$

**User Example**:

* Deposit = \$1,000 into 180d pool.
* At maturity → \$1,043.80.
* If exit at day 45:

  * Accrued = \$1,010.6
  * With 1% penalty → \$1,000.5 (basically break-even).

---

## 9. Display to Users

When selecting a lock:

**Input**: \$1,000, Lock = 180d
**Output (UI)**:

* Projected APY: 8.9%
* Projected return: \$1,043.80
* Early exit allowed after 30 days, with 1% penalty.

---

## 10. Summary of Strategy

* **Keep underlying pool diversified in short-term bills.**
* **Publish tenor-specific APYs** by pro-rating the average pool APY.
* **Show users projected returns at entry** (amount × APY).
* **Handle early exits** via daily accrual × penalty.
* **Keep it consistent** across all pools (Tbills now, bonds/credit later).

---
